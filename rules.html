Age-Specific Rules

Open the section that applies to your team. Complete wording remains available in the official packet.

Coach Pitch7U–8U divisions+
  • Seven runs maximum per inning or three outs.
  • Everyone on the team must bat.
  • Six pitches per batter or three strikes; foul balls on the sixth pitch may extend the at-bat.
  • Bunting and the infield fly rule do not apply.
  • Base runners may not leave until the hitter makes contact.
  • No seeds, shells or gum are allowed on the field.
9U Closed Base60-foot bases · 46-foot pitching distance+
  • No lead-offs; runners may leave when the ball reaches home plate.
  • The infield fly rule is in effect.
  • Batters may not advance on a dropped third strike.
  • Runners may steal home and score on wild pitches or passed balls.
  • There are no balks in 9U Closed Base.
Arrive prepared

Parking & Coach Entry

Use the designated parking areas shown on the facility map. Coaches must use Entry #1 for free entry; Entry #2 may require payment.

Do not park in front of neighboring businesses. Vehicles in unauthorized areas may be towed at the owner's expense.
